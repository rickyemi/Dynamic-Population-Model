Builds the four one dimensional tipping point figures used in the DPM modelling report. Each figure
plots the difference in survivors against the swept transition probability, faceted by RERR and
gateway effect, with the mean curve and the two 95% posterior interval curves. Where a curve crosses
zero is its tipping point, interpolated between the two swept probabilities that bracket zero. The
diamond marks where the mean curve reaches the master model survivor value.

Outputs, all under the configured folder: ``color/`` and ``gray/`` figure sets in svg and png, a QC
table of every tipping point, a multipage PDF report with a metadata page, and a dated, versioned
export bundle with a manifest.
